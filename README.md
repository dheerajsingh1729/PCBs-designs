Communication Systems: Analog & Digital Circuitry



A collection of PCB designs and bench-verified circuits focusing on modulation techniques and signal generation.

📡 Modulation/Demodulation Trainer
Capabilities: Integrated PCB containing blocks for AM, PAM, FM, PWM, PPM, and ASK.

Signal Conditioning: Features dedicated Pre-emphasis and De-emphasis circuitry to demonstrate high-fidelity audio transmission.

Physical Prototyping: Transitioned from EDA layout to a fully populated trainer board for laboratory verification.

🎹 Oscillator Verification
RC Phase Shift Oscillator: Designed and fabricated a custom oscillator PCB.

Bench Validation: Captured 50 Hz and 1 kHz waveforms on digital oscilloscopes to verify frequency stability and phase integrity.
